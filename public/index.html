Why restring regularly?

Strings lose tension every time you play.

Even if they haven’t snapped, old strings go dead — and your game pays for it. Fresh strings give you back:

More control

Consistent string tension means a predictable string bed — and shots that land where you aim them.

Better power

Responsive strings return more energy into the shuttle, so your smashes hit harder for the same effort.

Injury prevention

Dead strings make you swing harder to compensate, putting extra strain on your shoulder, elbow and wrist.

Racket longevity

Correctly tensioned strings protect your frame from uneven stress, so your racket lasts longer.

How often?

A simple rule for your restring schedule.

The more you play, the faster your strings fade. If you can’t remember when you last restrung, it’s probably time.

Tournament & league players

Training several times a week or competing regularly.

Every 2–4 weeks

Club & casual players

Playing once or twice a week for fun and fitness.

Every 2–3 months
