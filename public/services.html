FAQ

Good questions.

Which string should I choose?

BG65 Titanium is a durable all-rounder that’s great value for club and social players. BG80 has a crisper, more powerful feel favoured by competitive players. Not sure? Add a note to your booking and we’ll help you pick.

What tension should I go for?

Most club players are comfortable around 22–24 lbs. Higher tensions give more control but a smaller sweet spot; lower tensions give more power and comfort. If you’re unsure, write “not sure” and we’ll recommend one based on your game — always within your racket’s recommended range.

How does drop-off and collection work?

When you book, choose either Etwall Leisure Centre or 5 Egginton Road. We’ll get in touch to agree a time, then you collect your freshly strung racket from the same place. It’s completely free.

Do you post rackets?

No — to keep your racket safe and our prices low, all orders are dropped off and collected in person at one of our two local points.

Can I supply my own strings?

Yes. Choose “My own strings” when booking and we’ll quote you for labour only.

How do I pay?

No payment is taken online. You’ll pay when you collect your racket.
